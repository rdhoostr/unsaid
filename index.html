WHAT IT HOLDS

Three permanent, honest layers.

Every confession is stored directly on BOT Chain smart contracts. That means no server can go down, no one can censor it, and nothing can be quietly changed after the fact.

✦

Anonymous authorship

Your wallet signs each post to prove it is real, but your name and identity are never revealed. Just enough to stop spam, private enough to be fully honest.

✦

Permanent record

BOT Chain stores confessions on a public ledger that no one can change. Once posted, it stays exactly as written forever. Not even the developer can delete it.

✦

Quiet reactions

React to confessions with a simple tap. Each reaction is recorded on BOT Chain instantly. No comment threads, no drama, just a quiet signal that you felt something too.

HOW IT WORKS

Three steps, one wallet.

1

Connect your wallet +

No account, email, or password needed. Just connect your wallet (like MetaMask). Your wallet address proves you are a real person without revealing who you are.

2

Write what you've never said +

Up to 280 characters. A regret, a secret, or a truth you've held inside. Published with near-zero gas fees and sub-second confirmation on BOT Chain.

3

Read, and react quietly +

Browse confessions from people around the world. Tap a reaction to show you felt it too. BOT Chain confirms it in under a second with near-zero cost.
